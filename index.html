Why touchAble

Gone are the days
of being chained to your DAW.

We work passionately since 2009 to make music touchable — to give you the freedom to create. touchAble puts your whole Live set in your hands: effortless, at home, in the studio, during a live gig. Our apps will set you free.

Read our story

Every part of Live.
Every part in touch.

Six modules, one app — mirrored from your set in real time.

Mixer

Volume, pan, sends, all of it.

Real faders with real feel. Mute, solo, arm and record — a flick of the thumb, live meters on every strip.

Session

Launch clips like the pros.

The Session view on your screen — every clip, every scene, every stop button exactly where you expect it. Punch in, overdub, let it ride.

Devices

Your plugins, mapped to glass.

Live's built‑in devices and your plugins, laid out as knobs, faders and switches — with dedicated templates for the ones you use most.

Keys

Play. Not click.

A full piano keyboard with chords, scales and intervals — strum an idea, walk a bassline, feel the part before you record it.

XY

Sweep parameters with two fingers.

Map any two parameters to an XY field, ride them live, record a move and let the ball replay it in time with Live. Multi‑touch morphing, straight from the app you already love.

Editor

Build your own instrument.

The template editor lets you place every control, size it to your fingers and map it to anything in Live. Edit notes too — the piano roll comes with it.

Low latency

As fast as your
hands are.

Direct connection over Wi‑Fi or a single cable — nothing but control data on the wire: your moves go to Live's own API, Live's state comes back as it happens, and audio never enters the picture. A fader move and a meter jump land in the same instant. What you touch is what you hear.
